This page illustrates what a GDPR-compliant privacy policy looks like for a Belgian bank; it is not legal advice.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 July 2026

1. Data controller

For the purposes of the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") and the Belgian Act of 30 July 2018 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data, the data controller is Bank of Belgium, presented for this exercise as established in Belgium. You can reach us via the Support page.

2. What personal data we collect

  • Identity data: first name, last name, date of birth, username.
  • Contact data: e-mail address, phone number, postal address.
  • Financial data: account balances, IBANs, transaction history, card details (encrypted at rest).
  • Security data: login history, IP addresses, audit logs, and security events, used to detect fraud and unauthorised access.

3. Why we process your data and the legal basis

Purpose Legal basis (Art. 6 GDPR)
Opening and operating your account, processing transfers and deposits Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b))
Verifying your age and identity, keeping records for audit purposes Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c))
Fraud prevention, security monitoring, and the SOC dashboard Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f))
Sending you a one-time code for e-mail verification or password reset Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b))

4. How long we keep your data

We keep account and transaction records for as long as your account is active, and, where a real bank would be subject to Belgian anti-money-laundering record-keeping obligations, for a further period after account closure. Security and audit logs are retained only as long as needed for the purposes described above.

5. Who we share your data with

We do not sell your personal data. In this demo, no data leaves the application; in a production banking context, data may be shared with regulators, payment infrastructure providers, or law enforcement where legally required.

6. Your rights under the GDPR

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data (via your profile page).
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten"), subject to any legal retention obligations.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing.
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format (e.g. your transaction history or account statement).
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.

To exercise these rights, contact us via the Support page.

7. Complaints to the supervisory authority

You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Autorité de protection des données / Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit):

Autorité de protection des données (APD/GBA)
Rue de la Presse 35, 1000 Brussels, Belgium
autoriteprotectiondonnees.be

8. Security

We protect your data using measures such as password hashing, multi-factor authentication, encryption of sensitive fields, CSRF protection, and continuous monitoring via our Security Operations Center. No system is perfectly secure, and we encourage you to also keep your own credentials confidential.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated to you before they take effect.
